# GB ICO Data-Protection Fee Assessment

- **Controller:** 7BE Inc., trading as B4GAMBLE
- **Assessment date:** 19 August 2026
- **Status:** BLOCKED — EXTERNAL ACTION REQUIRED
- **Owner:** Founder / Finance, reviewed by Legal

## Decision

The fee is required. 7BE Inc. is an electronic-data controller within the territorial scope of UK GDPR and uses personal data for account, Programme, support, security and service-delivery purposes that are not confined to the listed core-purpose exemptions. The external action is registration/payment and selection of the correct tier using current corporate facts.

## Evidence classification

### Detected

- The application processes identity, account, session, IP/user-agent, Contact, Programme authority/progress/confirmed output and security data electronically.
- The controller decides the purposes, data fields, providers, retention approach and rights process.
- The service promotes third-party gambling operators and may later use clearly labelled affiliate links, while current GB commercial actions remain off.
- No ICO registration reference, payment receipt, tier decision or renewal date is present in the repository.

### Inferred

- Exemptions limited to staff administration, own advertising/marketing/PR, accounts and records, not-for-profit purposes and the other published categories do not cover the full processing described above.
- The overseas-controller position does not remove the fee where UK GDPR territorial scope applies.

### Planned

- Complete the ICO registration with exact legal entity/address, current average worldwide staff count and annual turnover.
- Pay the applicable fee, retain the receipt/certificate/reference outside the public repository and assign an annual renewal owner and reminder.
- Add the registration fact to the legal evidence register only after the ICO record is visible and reconciled.

### Not detected

- Current worldwide turnover, average members of staff, public-authority/charity/small-pension status, payment method or prior registration. These are corporate facts that cannot be inferred from code.

## Current tier rules

| Tier | Current test | Annual fee |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Maximum annual turnover £632,000 **or** no more than 10 members of staff | £52 |
| 2 | Not Tier 1, with maximum annual turnover £36 million **or** no more than 250 members of staff | £78 |
| 3 | Does not meet Tier 1 or Tier 2 | £3,763 |

The ICO treats Tier 3 as the default until the controller supplies facts supporting a lower tier. Direct Debit currently reduces the fee by £5. Staff outside the UK count.

## Execution checklist

1. Founder/Finance supplies the completed financial-year turnover and average worldwide staff calculation.
2. Legal confirms no special-category exception changes the tier and records the required-fee conclusion.
3. Complete the [ICO registration service](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/register/) and payment.
4. Retain certificate/reference, public-register link, paid date, tier, renewal date and named owner.
5. Reassess on material controller, turnover, staff or processing change and at renewal.
