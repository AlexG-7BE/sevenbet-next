## LEGAL-IMPL-01 outcome boundary

The workstream may be called **INTERNAL LEGAL/PRIVACY REMEDIATION IMPLEMENTED** only after its exact-head CI, Preview QA, no-JavaScript, accessibility, responsive and smoke gates pass. It does not mean outside counsel approved launch, ICO/UK-representative actions are complete, processor transfers are verified, recovery is proven, a real partner exists or affiliate traffic is authorised.

## Closed commercial beta ceiling

Any future Founder-authorised beta is limited to GB, 1–3 manually approved operators, manual licence/domain/offer verification and server-authoritative redirects. It excludes paid media, marketing email/SMS, retargeting, AI commercial targeting, white label and vulnerability-driven selection. LEGAL-IMPL-01 does not activate that ceiling.
