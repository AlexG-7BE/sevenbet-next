# RFC-006 — Human Guidance Trust-Led Design Direction

## Decision summary

SevenBet SHALL adopt **Human Guidance** as its active product and brand design direction.

The direction presents SevenBet as a calm, credible and useful personal control product with a transparent regulated-discovery business around it. The 10-Step Control Program is the central product object and the only conversion action inside the Home content. `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, `Best offers`, reviews and comparisons remain persistent, plainly labelled routes in the global header and footer; the Home body does not contain an offer preview, commercial bridge or affiliate CTA.

This decision supersedes **Prismatic Product Theatre** as future visual authority. Prismatic demonstrated useful text restraint and a strong product-visible Hero, but its dark spatial theatre, refractive effects and futuristic identity did not provide the required level of human and institutional trust for SevenBet.

The selected direction is based on the second approved Home exploration, **Human Guidance**, with two founder amendments: the final Home SHALL contain materially less copy than the exploration, and its narrative SHALL sell the Program through self-recognition, evidence, a visible practical result and an accessible Help route. The Hero may retain the successful Prismatic level of text density; every section below it must become faster to scan and more selective.

This RFC changes visual, content-density and Home composition direction only. It does not change the Product Vision, Program registration sequence, reward restrictions, protected Help experience, UK market rules, affiliate disclosure requirements or commercial eligibility rules.

## 1. Product expression

The design must make three product truths clear without mixing them into competing Home actions:

1. **A useful Program:** the user performs short missions and creates a personal control plan.
2. **A trustworthy decision service:** SevenBet shows licensing, material terms, methodology, review dates and uncertainty.
3. **A transparent commercial business:** eligible casinos, bonuses and best offers remain directly accessible through the global header and footer, outside the Home conversion narrative.

The Program is not a decorative responsibility message placed around an affiliate catalogue. Commercial discovery is not hidden behind Program completion, but it is not promoted inside the Home Hero or lower Home sections. Persistent header and footer navigation are the approved Home-level access points for commercial discovery.

Approved Hero intent:

> Build a practical plan for more control.

Approved Home conversion action:

> Start the 10-Step Program

The action may be repeated in the Hero and final action block, but its label, destination and visual hierarchy SHALL remain consistent. Home has no secondary conversion CTA.

Required evidence limitation:

> SevenBet draws on methods studied in gambling-harm treatment and behaviour-change research. The complete programme has not yet been clinically evaluated.

Final UK-facing wording remains subject to content and compliance review.

## 2. Trust thesis

Human Guidance builds trust through visible behaviour rather than claims.

The interface should feel:

- calm without becoming passive;
- human without relying on generic lifestyle advertising;
- serious without resembling a bank portal;
- commercially capable without resembling a casino lobby;
- supportive without presenting SevenBet as medical treatment;
- contemporary without spatial spectacle or speculative technology cues.

Trust is demonstrated through five recurring interface behaviours:

1. the next useful action is obvious;
2. the user can see what a mission produces;
3. material commercial facts appear before referral activation;
4. review dates, sources and affiliate status are visible;
5. private Program and safety information are not repurposed for promotional pressure.

The page must not imply that SevenBet, its Program or its content is approved by NHS or NICE, or that the complete Program is clinically proven.

## 3. Reference lock

### 3.1 Primary reference role — Fruitful

Fruitful is the primary reference for approachable financial-product trust, restrained green action colour, generous light space and a relationship-oriented product tone.

Traits to preserve:

- warm light canvas rather than pure white or a dark void;
- deep green as a controlled action and verification colour;
- spacious product compositions with one dominant next step;
- friendly but precise typography;
- rounded product panels used selectively;
- quiet supporting colour rather than a promotional gradient system.

### 3.2 Supporting reference role — Function Health

Function Health contributes evidence seriousness and the balance between warm editorial surfaces and structured product information.

Details that may be adapted:

- parchment and cream surface hierarchy;
- a restrained serif for short display statements;
- practical sans-serif typography for controls and evidence;
- short expert or methodology proof placed near the relevant decision;
- comfortable reading rhythm without long marketing paragraphs.

### 3.3 Journey reference role — Brilliant

Brilliant contributes concrete Program behaviour rather than brand styling:

- one clear mission at a time;
- visible progress;
- task completion before advancement;
- useful saved outcome;
- concise reward feedback;
- a clear return destination in the personal Dashboard.

### 3.4 Explicit rejects

The design SHALL reject:

- casino neon, dark gambling-lobby styling and jackpot visual language;
- futuristic cores, spectral glass, holographic panels and decorative WebGL theatre;
- generic smiling-person Hero photography;
- large volumes of explanatory copy on Home;
- repeated cards containing the same proposition in different words;
- more than one Home conversion action;
- oversized icon grids and generic feature bento layouts;
- rewards attached to deposits, operator registration, referrals or gambling activity;
- commercial personalisation derived from private Program, pause, Help or vulnerability data;
- unverified testimonials, invented participant quotes, testimonial-style avatars or outcome claims presented without the required evidence;
- an in-body casino, bonus, best-offer, commercial bridge or operator-preview section on Home.

## 4. Content-density contract

Home is a decision surface, not a complete explanation of SevenBet. A visitor should understand the Program's purpose, evidence basis, practical result and next action by scanning headings, product states and evidence labels. Commercial routes remain discoverable through the global header and footer without entering the Home narrative.

### 4.1 Hero

The Hero SHALL contain:

- one short kicker;
- one proposition of approximately 4–9 words;
- one supporting sentence;
- one Program conversion action;
- one concise trust or privacy line;
- one real Program product state.

The Hero should preserve the successful Prismatic text density: neither empty spectacle nor a long manifesto.

### 4.2 Sections below the Hero

Every lower Home section SHALL follow these limits unless compliance requires additional visible disclosure:

- one heading;
- at most one short supporting sentence;
- one primary visual or component group;
- no new conversion action or destination;
- no body paragraph intended to exceed two desktop lines or three mobile lines;
- no repeated explanation already communicated by the product visual;
- no card whose only purpose is to hold prose.

Home copy should use labels, values, material terms and action-oriented microcopy. Detailed education belongs in Program missions and guides. Detailed operator analysis belongs in reviews and comparisons. Full methodology belongs on its own route, with concise evidence and a visible utility link on Home.

### 4.3 Single-action contract

The only conversion action inside Home content is `Start the 10-Step Program`. It may appear in the Hero and final action block. These are repetitions of one action, not separate CTAs.

The following are navigation, evidence or safety utilities rather than competing conversion CTAs:

- global header and footer links to `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, `Best offers`, reviews and comparisons;
- account entry;
- source and methodology links;
- protected Help, self-exclusion, privacy and legal links.

Utility links SHALL use navigation or text-link styling, not the primary button treatment.

### 4.4 Claim discipline

The Home may explain what a user builds and what each Program phase contains. It SHALL not guarantee regained control, clinical improvement, recovery, reduced gambling or any other individual outcome. `Private`, `clinically evaluated`, `approved`, `proven` and equivalent trust claims may appear only when their exact scope is supported and approved.

## 5. Visual foundations

The initial foundation direction SHALL be tokenised and validated before becoming production authority.

### 5.1 Colour roles

| Role | Direction | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `surface/warm` | warm off-white | Primary public canvas. |
| `surface/sage` | pale desaturated green | Program Hero and calm product emphasis. |
| `surface/white` | clean opaque white | Mission, offer and evidence components. |
| `text/forest` | deep green-black | Primary text and high-authority chrome. |
| `text/secondary` | muted green-grey | Supporting copy and metadata. |
| `action/green` | deep accessible green | Primary CTA, progress and verified states. |
| `accent/peach` | restrained warm peach | Rare human warmth; decorative or educational emphasis only. |
| `accent/gold` | muted warm gold | XP and Program reward feedback only. |

Peach and gold are not commercial urgency colours. Green is not permission to imply that an operator is safe; verification labels must state exactly what was checked.

### 5.2 Typography

The initial type direction is:

- a readable editorial serif for short Hero and section headlines;
- a modern humanist sans-serif for navigation, controls, body copy and product UI;
- a compact mono or tabular treatment only where dates, evidence IDs or verification metadata benefit from it.

Display typography must not turn Home into a long editorial article. The product UI remains dominant.

### 5.3 Geometry and depth

- generous outer whitespace;
- soft but controlled 14–24px radii for major Program surfaces;
- smaller 8–14px radii for functional cards and controls;
- opaque product surfaces;
- thin quiet borders;
- subtle shadows reserved for the active product state;
- no glassmorphism or decorative glow;
- depth used to focus attention, not to make every section float.

## 6. Home architecture

The approved Home structure is a single Program conversion narrative:

1. **Global navigation** — `10-Step Program`, `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, `Best offers`, reviews, comparison, account and Help.
2. **Hero / active mission** — softened outcome proposition, one supporting sentence, the single Program CTA and one real Mission 01 preview.
3. **Static self-recognition** — `Is gambling becoming harder to control?`, followed by three concise signs adapted from themes in the NHS gambling self-assessment: spending or risking more than planned; returning to win back losses; and guilt, stress or problems affecting money or relationships. The block has no input, score, response control or CTA. It closes with: `One or more of these signs may be a reason to pause and start the 10-Step Program.` It is labelled as self-reflection, not diagnosis.
4. **Evidence basis** — four concise method roles: recognise patterns, set personal rules, create a pause and review or adjust. It includes the required limitation: `SevenBet draws on methods studied in gambling-harm treatment and behaviour-change research. The complete programme has not yet been clinically evaluated.`
5. **Program path and practical result** — combine the ten steps into `Recognise`, `Build` and `Apply`, and show the concrete artefacts that assemble the user's Personal Control Plan. This is one product composition, not ten explanatory cards.
6. **Plan-use scenarios** — under `How the plan can be used`, show concrete situations in which a person can apply a rule they created, without quotes, names, avatars or claims that real participants achieved particular outcomes. The block is labelled: `Illustrative examples of plan use, not participant outcomes or guaranteed results.`
7. **Methodology and sources** — concise source roles, publication or review dates, SevenBet content owner, applicable market and next review date. Source links are evidence utilities, not conversion CTAs.
8. **Final action and Help route** — repeat the single `Start the 10-Step Program` action. Present protected Help, self-exclusion and urgent-support access as visually separate safety links immediately before the footer.
9. **Footer** — product, commercial, editorial, legal, account and support routes, including a plain-language affiliate-business disclosure.

The final Home should feel complete through composition and product evidence, not through the number of sections or amount of copy.

## 7. Program-to-commercial boundary

The Home body does not create a narrative transition from possible gambling harm to an operator, offer or bonus. Persistent header and footer navigation provide independent access to commercial discovery and satisfy the Home-level route required by RFC-003. No in-body commercial CTA, offer preview or commercial bridge is required or permitted by this RFC.

Allowed:

- direct access to all eligible commercial discovery routes;
- commercial navigation in the global public and private shells as defined by RFC-003;
- user-initiated, non-sensitive comparison filters selected inside discovery;
- explanation of how control tools and material terms can be compared;
- visible operator control capabilities as factual comparison criteria;
- a voluntary transition from Dashboard to discovery.

Not allowed:

- “matches your personal plan” ranking based on stored Program answers;
- promotional targeting based on cues, limits, pauses, risk signals or Help activity;
- increased commercial pressure after mission completion;
- XP, achievements or streak rewards for commercial clicks or operator actions;
- export of Program, Help, pause, self-check or vulnerability events to advertising audiences, lookalike modelling, offer ranking or affiliate optimisation;
- correlation of identifiable Program or vulnerability data with commercial conversion data for individual targeting.

Program and vulnerability data SHALL NOT be used in advertising targeting. Product, commercial and trust/safety measurement remain separated according to RFC-003 and RFC-002; any permitted analysis is aggregated and privacy-preserving.

## 8. Evidence, outcomes and testimonial governance

### 8.1 Evidence claims

Every Home evidence statement must map to the specific Program method it informs. A bibliography alone does not prove the complete Program. Evidence items require a named source, source URL or controlled reference, publication/review date, applicable market and language, content owner, next review date and recorded clinical/content approval before publication.

NHS and NICE sources may be cited accurately but SHALL not be presented as SevenBet endorsement, approval, partnership or validation. Their logos or trust marks require separate documented permission.

The initial UK Home source baseline is:

| Source | Approved Home role | Required limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [NICE NG248 — Gambling-related harms: identification, assessment and management](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng248) (published 28 January 2025) | Context for identification, assessment, support and evidence-based psychological approaches. | NICE has not reviewed or approved the complete SevenBet Program. |
| [NHS — Help for problems with gambling](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/addiction-support/gambling-addiction/) (page last reviewed 8 December 2023; next review due 8 December 2026) | Themes for the static self-recognition signs and authoritative UK Help routes. | The SevenBet three-sign block is not the NHS nine-question assessment and is not diagnostic. |
| [Effect of cognitive-behavioral techniques for problem gambling and gambling disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37381589/) (2023) | Evidence context for trigger recognition, cognitive and behavioural methods. | Short-term findings, heterogeneity and uncertain follow-up effects must not be converted into a whole-Program efficacy claim. |
| [Psychological intervention for gambling disorder: a systematic review and meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37450372/) (2023) | Evidence context for structured psychological and behavioural intervention components. | Evidence for intervention categories does not validate SevenBet's specific composition. |
| [A Self-Guided Internet-Based Intervention for Gambling Problems](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11193125/) (2024) | Evidence context for structured digital self-guided support. | One trial does not establish that the SevenBet Program produces the same outcomes. |

This table is a candidate evidence baseline, not publication approval. The clinical/content review defined by RFC-002 must confirm source interpretation, applicable wording, owner and review cadence before the Home is released.

### 8.2 Home outcome block before testimonial evidence exists

Until SevenBet holds the approved testimonial evidence package, Home SHALL not display participant reviews or imitate them. The safe replacement is `How the plan can be used`: neutral, third-person or action-led examples showing how a Personal Control Plan can be applied in a concrete situation.

Permitted illustrative examples include:

- noticing the moment that usually precedes another deposit;
- using a defined pause before making another decision;
- following a prewritten action when a personal limit is reached.

These examples SHALL not use quotation marks, first-person speech, names, initials, portraits, star ratings or `real participant` labels. The page SHALL not say or imply that the Program can lead the user to particular testimonials or guaranteed behavioural outcomes.

### 8.3 Future participant testimonials

Real participant testimonials may replace or supplement the illustrative block only after SevenBet holds, for every item: the original quote; participant identity and contact record; proof of participation in the named Program version; informed permission for the intended media and duration; date and context; any incentive or commercial interest; approved edits; withdrawal process; and privacy or special-category-data assessment where applicable.

Testimonials remain individual experiences and do not substantiate objective efficacy claims. Their wording must not be strengthened, taken out of context or converted into a promise of recovery, treatment or regained control.

## 9. Responsive and accessibility behaviour

On mobile:

- the proposition and primary action appear before the Program preview;
- the Program preview becomes one compact vertical task state;
- trust evidence wraps into short rows rather than prose cards;
- the Program path becomes an ordered progression;
- plan-use scenarios remain visibly distinct from testimonials;
- commercial navigation remains available in the compact header and footer without entering the Hero;
- Help remains one clear, protected route.

All text and controls must meet applicable contrast, focus, keyboard and screen-reader requirements. Reduced content density must not remove required disclosure or make evidence available only through hover or animation.

## 10. Motion and imagery

Motion is functional and restrained:

- progress movement;
- task completion;
- saved-plan confirmation;
- lightweight section and state transitions;
- reduced-motion equivalents.

No looping spectacle, floating decorative objects or commercial celebration is permitted.

The Hero should be product-led. Human photography may appear later in educational or expert-review contexts only when it is real, relevant and properly art-directed. Generic stock portraits are not a substitute for trust.

## 11. Figma migration and delivery order

Implementation in Figma SHALL proceed as follows:

1. preserve Prismatic screens as superseded historical artefacts until the Human Guidance replacement is accepted;
2. create a separate Human Guidance Home working area without silently recolouring Prismatic components;
3. define the minimum colour, type, spacing, radius and elevation proposal needed by Home;
4. build the Hero and Mission 01 product preview with the single Program CTA first;
5. build and validate the static self-recognition and evidence sections;
6. combine the Program path and Personal Control Plan into one product composition;
7. add plan-use scenarios, methodology, final action and protected Help sections one at a time;
8. validate the continuous desktop Home, including the absence of in-body commercial promotion;
9. create and validate the mobile interpretation;
10. reconcile reusable components and foundations only after the Home direction is accepted;
11. move validated artefacts to Ready for Dev and archive superseded work deliberately.

No legacy or Prismatic foundation should be deleted before the accepted replacement can be audited against it.

## 12. Acceptance criteria

The direction is accepted only when:

- the Hero communicates SevenBet, the Program, its practical nature and the single Program action without scrolling;
- a first-time visitor can identify the primary next action within seconds;
- the Home contains visibly less copy than the selected exploration below the Hero;
- each lower section communicates one idea without introducing another conversion destination;
- the 10-Step Program looks like an interactive product, not responsible-gambling editorial content;
- self-recognition remains static, collects no answers, produces no score and is non-diagnostic;
- the evidence limitation is visible near the evidence claim;
- no review or participant outcome is presented without the required testimonial evidence package;
- commercial discovery remains directly accessible in the header and footer but does not appear in the Home body;
- affiliate business disclosure is present without becoming a promotional module;
- Program and vulnerability data are prohibited from advertising targeting and individual commercial personalisation;
- the page feels trustworthy without looking clinical, bureaucratic or futuristic;
- desktop and mobile preserve the same hierarchy;
- protected Help is visible immediately before the footer and remains commercially isolated.

## 13. Consequences

Human Guidance reduces the cost and risk of original spatial media while raising the standard for typography, product-state design, content editing and evidence quality. The single-action Home increases clarity and lowers the risk of placing commercial promotion next to possible gambling-harm recognition. It also makes the quality of the Program proposition, evidence mapping and visible product result responsible for conversion rather than an offer grid.

The design may appear quieter than Prismatic, but it must not become generic. Its memorable quality comes from the visible relationship between self-recognition, a human-scale mission, evidence-informed methods and a useful saved outcome. Commercial discovery remains a transparent SevenBet business layer in navigation and dedicated routes, not a Home conversion block.
