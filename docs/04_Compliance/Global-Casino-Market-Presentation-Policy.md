# Global Casino Market Presentation Policy

## Scope and authority

Reviewed 3 September 2026 for `GEO-LANGUAGE-GLOBAL-CATALOG-01`.

This is a bounded B4GAMBLE public-presentation policy for a globally published
Casino identity under the visitor's trusted request market. It is not legal
advice, a licence determination, partner acceptance, promotional approval or
affiliate-route authority. Exact current facts and all existing jurisdiction,
operator, partner, offer, tracking and redirect gates remain cumulative.

`YES` below means only that neutral identity/information may remain public in
that specific state. It never means that promotion or an outbound link is
permitted. `NO` means fail closed to `HIDDEN` until a later current evidence
review expressly changes the policy.

## Deterministic disposition

- A demo/synthetic/non-public identity is always `HIDDEN`.
- Contradictory exact-market evidence is `HIDDEN`.
- `PROHIBITED`, `NOT_LICENSED` and `BLOCKED` are `HIDDEN`.
- A missing, `UNKNOWN` or `UNAVAILABLE` exact profile follows the matrix above.
- `AVAILABLE` with no fully governed exact-country route is
  `INFORMATIONAL_ONLY`; promotional terms and actions are stripped.
- `PROMOTABLE` requires the exact available profile plus every independent
  jurisdiction, operator, legal/advertising, partner, offer, tracking-country,
  `productionEligible`, redirect and runtime kill-switch gate.

The current repository jurisdiction policy contains no passing public
commercial route. A synthetic test authority proves branching behavior only;
it does not create live authority.

## Review and change control

Recheck the exact official source and operator/domain record before any
commercial activation. Reopen this matrix for a material legal change, new
market, new promotional surface, new evidence that changes neutral-information
safety, or a contradiction. Do not mass-enable countries from language
publication, network coverage or a universal tracking URL.
