# Product Vision

## Purpose

Holds the constitutional product direction for SevenBet.

## What documents belong here

- Approved Product Vision & Principles and future major vision versions.
- Vision change records when a future Version 2.0 is approved.

## When this folder should be updated

Only when the company's fundamental product philosophy is formally changed.

The approved Version 1.0 is maintained at [Product Vision & Principles](../Product-Vision-and-Principles.md).
