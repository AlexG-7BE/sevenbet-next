# RFC-003 — Program-Led Commercial Growth

## Decision summary

SevenBet SHALL market the **10-Step Control Program** as the distinctive public reason to discover the brand. The Program is the product story; regulated casino discovery, bonuses, reviews, comparisons and affiliate referrals are the commercial product layer that users can access throughout the SevenBet experience.

The product SHALL not conceal its commercial role. `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, and `Best offers` are persistent, plainly labelled navigation in public and private product shells. The Program's XP, achievements and streaks are an engagement system for defined Program actions only. They neither unlock, improve, personalise nor reward an affiliate opportunity.

The dedicated Protected Help experience remains an exception: it contains no casino, bonus, best-offer or affiliate CTA.

## 1. Positioning and campaign promise

The approved campaign territory is **"10 Steps before you choose."**

Core promise:

> Compare casinos, understand offers, build your own rules.

Permitted supporting messages include:

- `10 Steps. Better gambling decisions.`
- `Before you choose a casino, choose your rules.`
- `Compare offers. Keep control.`
- `Your casino research deserves more than a bonus headline.`

Campaign copy must not promise safe gambling, clinical benefit, profit, winning, recovery of losses, or a bonus entitlement. Market-specific legal, age, disclosure and advertising approval remains mandatory before activation.

## 2. Acquisition model

### 2.1 Primary entry point

Brand and broad acquisition campaigns SHALL primarily lead to a dedicated Program landing page, not directly to an operator or bonus referral. The landing page must show:

1. the 10-mission map and the practical result a user can assemble;
2. that Mission 01 can begin without an account;
3. the private account benefit after Mission 01;
4. the Dashboard, XP, achievement and streak system;
5. a clearly separated route to `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, and `Best offers`.

### 2.2 Commercial acquisition

High-intent search, SEO, review and comparison routes may lead directly to a relevant commercial discovery page. Every such page must retain a visible route to the 10-Step Program and its relevant mission, for example a bonus explainer linking to the offer-check mission.

### 2.3 Content engine

Content is organised around the ten mission themes and commercial search intent: bonus terms, withdrawal rules, payment methods, personal limits, self-exclusion tools, licensing, comparison criteria and decision checklists. A content item routes to the associated mission, discovery/comparison experience, or both.

## 3. Product growth loops

| Loop | User path | Business role | Non-negotiable boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Acquisition | Search, paid media or editorial content → Program landing or discovery page | Brand reach and qualified traffic | 18+, jurisdiction and ad-policy controls apply. |
| Program retention | Mission → action result → XP / achievement / streak → Dashboard → next mission | Revisit and durable product engagement | No reward for gambling, deposit, referral or commercial click. |
| Trust-to-discovery | Program or editorial insight → voluntary comparison → eligible referral | Commercial conversion through informed discovery | Progress state must not change operator eligibility, ranking or offer quality. |

## 4. Navigation and surface rules

1. **Public shell:** Home, editorial, reviews, comparisons and discovery include `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, `Best offers`, `10 Steps`, `Help`, and account entry.
2. **Private Program shell:** authenticated Dashboard and Missions 02–10 include the same commercial navigation plus `Dashboard`, `My Plan`, and `Programme`. Program progress remains a distinct area of the page.
3. **Mission 01 / registration:** compact commercial navigation is permitted, but the primary action remains the current mission or saving its result.
4. **Protected Help:** no casino, bonus, best-offer, affiliate or promotional CTA. It may link back to a neutral public home route only after the help task is complete.
5. **Reward separation:** an earned XP toast, achievement, streak prompt or mission-completion screen cannot contain an operator referral, bonus card or "play now" CTA.

## 5. Commercial discovery rules

`Casinos`, `Bonuses`, and `Best offers` are core commercial routes. They SHALL:

- show applicable eligibility, material terms and affiliate/sponsorship disclosure before referral activation;
- use a documented ranking methodology rather than commission alone;
- explain why an offer is shown, restricted or unavailable;
- link to relevant Program missions where useful, without making Program completion a prerequisite;
- remain governed by market and referral decisions defined in RFC-001.

## 6. Measurement

Growth reporting SHALL keep product engagement and commercial performance separate, then analyse their relationship only at an aggregated level.

| Category | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| Acquisition | qualified landing visits, program-start rate, discovery engagement, market-eligible referral rate |
| Program | mission-action completion, XP earned from Program actions, achievement progression, voluntary streak return, Dashboard return |
| Commercial | qualified referral activation, partner revenue, offer-page performance, comparison-to-referral rate |
| Trust and safety | disclosure visibility, help-route use, complaint rate, content freshness, stop-signal review |

No commercial experiment, ranking system, audience segment or lifecycle message may use pause status, Help use, personal boundaries, mission answers, reflections, XP, achievements or streaks to increase promotional pressure.

## 7. Delivery requirements

1. Create the Program acquisition landing page and campaign visual system.
2. Build shared public and private headers with the approved navigation.
3. Build the commercial discovery widget for Dashboard, visually separate from Program progress and mission rewards.
4. Add a contextual Program entry module to reviews, bonus explainers and comparisons.
5. Implement measurement with separate product, commercial and trust/safety datasets.
6. Obtain jurisdiction-specific compliance approval for each paid campaign and commercial claim before launch.

## 8. Consequences

This decision makes SevenBet commercially legible and gives paid, search and editorial acquisition a differentiated message beyond a generic casino catalogue. It does not convert Program activity into gambling eligibility, commercial reward or promotional targeting. Any proposal to do so requires a new Product, Compliance and legal decision; it is not authorised by this RFC.
