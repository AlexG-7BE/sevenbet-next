# RFC-010 — Mission 04 Build One Boundary

> **Persistence supersession — 2026-08-09:** [RFC-017](RFC-017-GB-Legal-Privacy-and-Launch-Remediation.md) supersedes this RFC only for user-authored boundary narrative. Trigger, rule, unit/period wording, execution detail and coping text are browser-session local; the server retains only RFC-017's bounded structured fields and neutral legacy-column markers. Mission content, order, prerequisites, `+100 XP` and `Boundary built` remain unchanged.

## Status and decision boundary

This RFC was approved by the founder/product owner on 2026-08-04. It defines the product,
evidence, design, persistence and reward contract for Mission 04. The approved reward is
`+100 XP` with the zero-XP achievement `Boundary built`.

Clinical-content and compliance review remain release gates. Approval of this RFC does
not represent clinical evaluation of the SevenBet Programme or proof that a personal
boundary will prevent gambling-related harm.

Mission 04 SHALL turn the learner's existing Current Goal and, when intentionally saved,
early signal into one private rule chosen before a future decision point. The learner
SHALL define what activates the rule, what the rule requires, how it will be made easier
to follow and what happens if the learner wants to change it in the moment.

## Intended outcome

At completion, the learner owns an editable `ActiveBoundary` that answers four questions:

1. **When does it apply?** A concrete decision point, cue, early signal or scheduled time.
2. **What is the rule?** A specific money, time, access or pause boundary.
3. **How will it be carried out?** One practical enforcement or friction action.
4. **What if it feels difficult?** One coping action and an outside-the-moment review rule.

The mission is not complete after choosing a category or writing a vague intention. The
saved result must be specific enough that the learner can later tell whether the planned
action happened. SevenBet does not monitor gambling activity and does not claim that the
boundary was followed.

## Product rules

1. Mission 04 uses the RFC-002 `Build` interaction grammar, not a questionnaire.
2. A structured template may reduce writing load, but the learner chooses every material
   value. SevenBet SHALL NOT prefill or recommend a monetary amount.
3. The learner may choose one of four boundary categories:
   - `money`: a self-selected financial amount and clearly stated period;
   - `time`: a specific no-start, stop or review point;
   - `access`: a concrete block, removal or access restriction;
   - `pause`: a signal-triggered period before another decision.
4. The interface SHALL distinguish a boundary from a target, aspiration or prediction.
5. A boundary may use an operator, bank, device or self-exclusion control as its execution
   method, but Mission 04 SHALL remain provider-neutral and SHALL contain no operator,
   casino, bonus, offer or affiliate action.
6. A learner who is not ready to create a boundary may save the mission as paused and
   leave or use Help. Pausing is not failure and awards no completion reward.
7. Changing or deleting the saved boundary remains possible from the Dashboard. A future
   edit does not reverse truthful historical XP or completion facts.
8. Programme answers, boundary values, tool choices, pause state and review state SHALL
   NOT enter advertising targeting, commercial personalisation, ranking or affiliate
   analytics.

## Evidence boundary

Mission 04 MAY explain that pre-commitment means making a choice before a tempting or
high-pressure moment, and that concrete action and coping plans have been studied as ways
to support an existing intention.

Mission 04 SHALL also disclose that evidence for voluntary gambling limit tools is mixed.
A prompt may increase limit-setting without demonstrating reduced gambling intensity, and
a brief action-and-coping-planning trial did not improve adherence for the full sample.
The complete SevenBet mission has not been clinically evaluated.

Mission 04 SHALL NOT state or imply that:

- any money or time amount is safe;
- a self-set boundary guarantees adherence or prevents harm;
- a boundary makes a person ready or suitable to gamble;
- SevenBet can enforce an operator, bank, device or self-exclusion control;
- failure to follow a boundary is a moral or personal failure;
- Mission 04 provides CBT, treatment, financial advice or clinical assessment.

### Initial evidence register

SevenBet is not an operator and cannot set or enforce an operator-account limit. |
| [UK Gambling Commission, new deposit-limit rules](https://www.gamblingcommission.gov.uk/news/article/new-deposit-limit-rules-provide-clarity-for-consumers), 7 October 2025 | Use the regulated definition of a deposit limit and explain that requests to decrease a financial limit should be acted on immediately by operators. | Other financial-limit types differ; SevenBet must not label every financial boundary a deposit limit. |
| [Ivanova et al., deposit-limit prompt RCT](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6455077/), 2019 | Calibrate claims: prompting increased limit-setting but the trial did not find lower gambling intensity. | One operator context and design do not establish effectiveness or ineffectiveness of every pre-commitment tool. |

Every rendered evidence item requires a content version, owner, reviewer, last-reviewed
date, next-review date and market/language scope before production publication.

## Mission sequence

The target range is 20–25 minutes of active participation at a normal pace. Completion is
based on required work, never elapsed time.

| Screen | Stage | Required learner action | Immediate product response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Orient | Review the outcome, current Goal/early-signal context, privacy note and evidence limitation. | Shows the nine-part mission path and previews the four-part boundary result. |
| 02 | Learn | Assemble the four parts of a usable boundary: decision point, rule, execution method and coping/review plan. | Contrasts a vague intention with a concrete, reviewable rule and exposes sources. |
| 03 | Apply | Choose one category: money, time, access or pause. | Explains what the category can and cannot control; no amount or duration is preselected. |
| 04 | Apply | Connect the boundary to one decision point, saved early signal or neutral structured trigger. | Creates the `when` part without requiring new sensitive disclosure. |
| 05 | Build | Define the concrete rule and, where relevant, a self-selected value and period. | Produces a live plain-language boundary sentence and checks required specificity. |
| 06 | Build | Choose or write one execution method: account limit, bank block, device/site block, payment/access removal, trusted contact, leave action or protected self-exclusion route. | Shows what SevenBet can save and what must be activated outside SevenBet. |
| 07 | Build | Create one coping action for the moment the learner wants to override or change the rule, plus an outside-the-moment review point. | Adds a fallback to the boundary instead of treating intention as enforcement. |
| 08 | Review | Identify the stronger boundary in a neutral scenario and run the learner's draft through four strength checks. | Gives explained feedback with unlimited retry and no penalty; missing parts route back to the relevant builder. |
| 09 | Review | Review, edit and confirm the complete boundary, privacy controls, evidence limitation and next safe action. | Atomically saves `ActiveBoundary`, completes Mission 04, awards the deterministic recognition and makes Mission 05 current. |

Exiting after any accepted task SHALL preserve a resumable private draft. The mission
SHALL show completed work, not time spent.

## Boundary strength checks

The product checks structure, not whether the amount or choice is clinically or
financially appropriate:

1. **Placed before pressure:** the decision point is recognisable before or at the earliest
   saved signal.
2. **Specific:** the action, value/period where applicable and completion condition are
   clear.
3. **Made executable:** at least one practical control or friction action is named.
4. **Protected from in-the-moment editing:** a coping action and later review point are
   defined.

A check cannot label a boundary safe, healthy, sufficient or suitable for gambling.

## Interaction and visual contract

- Reuse the existing Tilt-Locked foundations, Program Header, Mission Progress, Task
  Option, Reflection Field, Evidence Note, Feedback Panel, Core Button, Recognition and
  Path Node components.
- Extend, rather than duplicate, `SevenBet / Program / Result Artifact` with an
  `Active Boundary` variant.
- The signature interaction is a live **Boundary Composer**: four horizontal layers lock
  into one plain-language rule as the learner completes them.
- Use the existing warm-paper canvas, black product surfaces, Archivo hierarchy and
  acid-yellow only for primary action/progress. Teal remains reserved for Help/verified
  safety meaning.
- Use human photography as situational context on orient, execution and completion
  screens, not as decoration or implied participant evidence.
- The mission body contains one principal action per screen and substantially less prose
  than a clinical form.
- Incorrect checks receive an explanation and retry. No countdown, streak-loss threat,
  leaderboard, random reward or commercial prompt is allowed.
- Desktop, mobile, keyboard, visible-focus, reduced-motion, paused, resume, edit, delete
  and validation-error states remain acceptance requirements.

## Completion and reward

Mission 04 completion requires:

1. the evidence item and its limitation were reviewed;
2. one boundary category and concrete decision point were selected;
3. a valid specific rule was built;
4. one execution method and one coping/review plan were saved;
5. the scenario check was answered correctly after any number of retries; and
6. the complete `ActiveBoundary` was reviewed and confirmed.

The proposed deterministic reward is `+100 XP` and the zero-XP achievement
`Boundary built`. The achievement recognises creation of the first editable boundary; it
does not claim adherence or behaviour change.

The Dashboard after completion SHALL show `330 XP` total, `4 of 10 complete`, Mission 05
as current, the editable active boundary, `Boundary built` as earned and the existing
truthful active-day state. Completing multiple missions on the same local day remains one
active day under RFC-008.

## Private data contract

The canonical aggregate is `ActiveBoundary`, owned through `ProgramEnrollment`:

- `missionVersion`
- `evidenceVersion`
- optional `sourceCurrentGoalId`
- optional `sourceUrgeLearningRecordId`
- `category`: `money | time | access | pause`
- `triggerType`
- optional `triggerText`
- `ruleText`
- optional `limitValue`
- optional `limitUnit`
- optional `limitPeriod`
- `executionMethod`
- optional `executionDetail`
- `copingAction`
- `reviewAt`
- `status`: `active | paused | retired`
- timestamps and deletion marker

Free text and user-selected values remain within the private Programme aggregate. They
SHALL NOT be copied to reward/progress metadata, logs, errors, advertising, commercial
profiles, ranking, recommendation logic, affiliate events or operator partners.

Deletion erases personal content without rewriting truthful mission, XP, achievement or
active-day history. Dashboard read, edit and delete routes require authenticated
ownership and `Cache-Control: no-store`.

## API and idempotency contract

The proposed authenticated routes are:

- `GET | PATCH /api/program/missions/04` for resumable draft state;
- `POST /api/program/missions/04/complete` for atomic validation, save, reward and unlock;
- `PATCH | DELETE /api/program/artefacts/active-boundary` for owner-controlled edits and
  erasure.

Completion SHALL run in the existing serializable transaction/retry boundary. Mission,
artefact, XP, achievement, progress-event and active-day keys are deterministic so a retry
cannot duplicate recognition or advance Mission 05 twice.

## Reference lock and decision ledger

### Primary direction

The existing Tilt-Locked/Empower system remains the visual authority: high-contrast
paper/night rhythm, heavy product typography, restrained acid action, real photography
and object-like product UI. Mission 04 does not introduce a new visual system.

### Bounded reference contributions

- Refero `Roots — Set Phone Pickups Budget` owns the journey pattern: overview → choose
  preset/custom structure → set value → confirm → updated home. SevenBet adapts the flow,
  not its visual tokens or default values.
- Refero `Cofounder — Flow Editor` owns the plan-preview pattern: preview what the rule
  will do, edit the plan, then return to a clearly enabled saved result.
- Refero `Copilot budget preview` contributes the review-before-save hierarchy only.
- Refero `Superpower` contributes compact evidence/meta treatment only; its vermillion
  accent does not enter SevenBet.

| Decision | Source | Role preserved | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Four-part live Boundary Composer | RFC-002 Build grammar + action/coping planning research | Product interaction | Makes the result executable rather than a category choice. |
| Preset structure plus user-defined material values | Roots flow + UKGC anti-anchoring guidance | Journey/content | Reduces writing load without suggesting a financial amount. |
| Reviewable enabled result on Dashboard | Cofounder flow + RFC-002 Dashboard | State transition | Makes the saved outcome visible and editable. |

Rejected patterns: generic survey pages, equal card grids, prefilled financial values,
purple/glow UI, medical dashboards, reward confetti, casino imagery and Help/commercial
action adjacency.

## Acceptance gates

Implementation and release require:

- explicit founder/product approval of this RFC, `+100 XP` and `Boundary built`;
- clinical-content review of every educational statement and scenario;
- compliance review of UK financial-limit terminology, Help/self-exclusion placement and
  the boundary between SevenBet guidance and operator controls;
- Figma approval for desktop and mobile orient, builder, scenario, validation, pause,
  resume, completion, Dashboard, edit and delete states;
- accessible implementation with keyboard order, visible focus, non-colour-only feedback
  and reduced-motion behaviour;
- additive migration, owner-authorised API, deterministic reward and idempotency tests;
- proof that private Programme data remains outside commercial analytics and targeting;
- connected-database browser verification from Mission 03 Dashboard through Mission 04
  completion to Dashboard `4 of 10`.

## Consequences

Mission 04 becomes the first fully compositional `Build` mission. It establishes a
reusable grammar for Mission 06 friction actions, Mission 09 when/then rules and Mission
10 plan review while preserving one user-owned result per mission.

The proposed implementation target is:

```text
Dashboard 3 of 10
  → Mission 04 nine-screen Boundary Composer
  → +100 XP + Boundary built
  → Dashboard 330 XP / 4 of 10
  → Mission 05 current
```
