## Reusable SevenBet application pack

### Repository-supported statements

- **Product:** SevenBet is an information, education, casino-review and comparison publisher. It is not a gambling operator.
- **Positioning:** the product is regulated-first; information, licensing, material terms and responsible-gambling context precede optional commercial action.
- **Intended initial commercial market:** Great Britain, subject to contract, LEGAL-02 and separate activation approval.
- **Editorial method:** partner compensation cannot change Editor Score, publication or operator eligibility; editorial and commercial authorities are separate.
- **Disclosure:** a public Affiliate Disclosure surface explains that SevenBet may receive compensation from qualifying outbound actions.
- **Responsible-gambling surfaces:** a Responsible Gambling hub, bonus/licence/payment education, Self-Check, Personal Gambling Limit Tracker and Protected Help content exist. Protected/safety data is not used for commercial targeting.
- **Educational Programme:** SevenBet includes an educational decision-support Programme designed to help users reflect on gambling behaviour, boundaries and choices. It is not treatment, therapy, a clinical intervention or a recovery guarantee.
- **Technical handoff:** external destinations remain server-side behind an internal governed redirect; request-time policy, operator, partner, offer, link and safety checks fail closed.
- **Current channels:** owned web editorial/review/comparison content. No email, SMS, push, retargeting, paid-ad tracking or affiliate pixel expansion is implemented by COMM-01.
- **Current launch state:** editorial product surfaces are deployed; GB commercial/referral policy and redirect engine remain off. No real partner is active.
- **Current audience/traffic:** **Unknown; Founder evidence required.** No 7BE marketplace metric may be reused as SevenBet data.

When real evidence exists, Founder Office may compare programmes using:

- CPA revenue per 1,000 eligible clicks = `1,000 × registration rate × FTD rate × approval rate × CPA`;
- RevShare revenue per 1,000 eligible clicks = `1,000 × registration rate × FTD rate × expected contract-defined NGR per FTD × RevShare %`;
- Hybrid = the separately calculated CPA and RevShare components;
- break-even attributable acquisition/operating cost = expected reconciled revenue per 1,000 eligible clicks;
- cash collection lag = weighted time from eligible click to qualified event, statement approval and payment; and
- confidence = evidence quality for every input, reduced to low when any material input is assumed or unknown.

Assumptions must be labelled and sensitivity-tested; they do not become facts or public ranking inputs.
