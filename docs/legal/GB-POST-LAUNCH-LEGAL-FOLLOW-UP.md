## Required review cadence

- P0 items: weekly until closed and before any related activation.
- P1 items: monthly until closed; support links quarterly after closure.
- Public legal pages: review on any provider, purpose, data flow, retention, commercial-model or law change, and at least every six months.
- Each closure entry must link to durable evidence, name an approver and record the date. An issue or meeting note alone is not closure evidence.

## Current primary-source baseline

- ICO: right to be informed, special-category conditions and international transfers.
- UK GDPR Article 27: representatives of controllers or processors not established in the UK.
- CAP Code Section 2 and Section 16; ASA/CAP guidance on free bets and bonuses.
- Gambling Commission: affiliates/third parties, advertising rules and GAMSTOP guidance.
- NHS: gambling support and urgent mental-health help.
- OpenAI official API data-controls documentation.

Re-check the current source before relying on it. Public-source review does not replace fact verification, contract review or outside-counsel advice.
