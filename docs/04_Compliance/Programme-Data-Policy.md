# Programme Data and Commercial-Separation Policy

Status: Approved implementation policy for RFC-008.

Programme answers, Moment Maps, Current Goals, Urge Learning Records, Active Boundaries, confidence, mission drafts, Help/pause context, XP, achievements and active-day state are sensitive personal-control data. Their permitted purposes are saving the user's requested plan, resuming the Programme, showing the private Dashboard, enforcing progress/reward integrity, honouring edit/deletion, and tightly scoped operations.

They are prohibited inputs to advertising audiences, operator or casino recommendations, bonuses, offer selection, affiliate optimisation, commercial eligibility, ranking and promotional pressure. No Programme route emits an affiliate/commercial event. No Help route is changed by this implementation.

Private content is stored only in anonymous drafts, authenticated mission drafts and user artefacts. Progress/reward ledgers contain completion facts and source IDs, not the text. API errors name invalid fields or states but do not echo values. Responses are private and non-cacheable.

Anonymous Mission 01 sessions expire after 24 hours of accepted activity; pending claims expire after 30 minutes. Automated purge is **Planned**, not detected. Artefact deletion immediately scrubs user-authored fields and hides the record while retaining a tombstone and non-content integrity references. Account-wide portability and erasure are **Planned** beyond this slice.

Programme analytics is **Not detected**. Commercial analytics is also **Not detected**. If either is introduced, they require distinct event contracts, stores/access policies and a prohibition on join keys that enable personal commercial targeting from Programme state.
