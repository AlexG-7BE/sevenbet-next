# RFC-007 — Tilt-Locked Human Product Theatre

## 1. Decision summary

SevenBet SHALL use **Tilt-Locked Human Product Theatre** as its active public visual direction. The reference lock is the structure, rhythm and art-direction mechanics observed on [Tilt](https://tilt.com/), not Tilt's protected brand assets, logo, copy or product claims.

The direction must feel like a confident 2026 consumer product rather than a responsible-gambling brochure, clinical portal or design-system documentation page. It combines:

- near-black cinematic surfaces;
- oversized mixed display typography;
- restrained acid-lime action colour;
- real, tightly art-directed human photography;
- full-viewport narrative chapters;
- product proof shown as high-impact UI theatre;
- white editorial sections with object-led cards;
- a compact social-proof composition when evidence is available.

This RFC changes visual expression and page composition only. Product Vision, UK launch-market rules, the 10-Step Program, Mission 01 registration sequence, reward restrictions, evidence limitations, protected Help, affiliate disclosure and the prohibition on vulnerability-data targeting remain unchanged.

## 2. Reference lock

### 2.1 Traits to preserve

- full-bleed dark opening scene with a persistent compact navigation layer;
- one enormous headline composed from a heavy grotesk and contrasting italic serif;
- several editorial photo crops placed around or through the headline rather than inside a conventional split hero;
- high negative space and confident scale instead of explanatory copy;
- a single acid-lime pill action used as the dominant interactive signal;
- horizontal product theatre with one sharply focused central product card and partially visible adjacent cards;
- white full-viewport statement break between product and human stories;
- cinematic full-bleed photography with short headline, one sentence and one action;
- white tool section with three object-led cards;
- social-proof grid mixing metrics, verified review evidence, editorial photography and a short participant quote only when the evidence pack exists;
- dark footer with a clear legal and disclosure layer.

### 2.2 SevenBet translation

Tilt's product categories translate into SevenBet content roles as follows:

| Tilt role | SevenBet role |
| --- | --- |
| Hero identity statement | The 10-Step Program as the clearest path to a personal control plan |
| Product carousel | Mission 01, Personal Control Dashboard and 10-Step path |
| Full-stop statement | Static self-recognition question and the reason to start |
| Full-bleed human chapters | Evidence-informed methods, plan-in-use situations and visible progress |
| Tools cards | Pause rule, trigger map and review routine |
| Social proof | Evidence sources, programme facts and approved participant evidence only |
| Sign-up CTA | `Start the 10-Step Program` |

Commercial discovery remains available through the persistent header and footer as `Casinos`, `Bonuses`, `Best offers`, reviews and comparison. It is not inserted into the harm-recognition narrative.

### 2.3 Explicit non-copy rule

SevenBet SHALL NOT copy Tilt's logo, wordmark, photographs, illustrations, product renders, copy, testimonials or distinctive branded assets. SevenBet may reproduce generic layout mechanics, scale relationships, section rhythm, card geometry, colour-role discipline and interaction patterns using original SevenBet content and properly licensed or generated media.

## 3. Visual foundations

### 3.1 Colour roles

| Token role | Direction | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `surface/night` | near-black `#100F0F` | Hero, product theatre, footer |
| `surface/ink-card` | charcoal `#242323` | Central product card and dark modules |
| `surface/paper` | warm white `#FAFAF7` | Statement, tools and evidence sections |
| `surface/white` | white `#FFFFFF` | Cards and product UI |
| `text/on-night` | warm white | Dark scenes |
| `text/ink` | near-black | Light scenes |
| `text/muted` | neutral grey | Supporting evidence and disclosures |
| `action/acid` | controlled lime `#E4E24E` | Primary CTA and active pagination only |
| `action/acid-soft` | pale lime | Secondary active surfaces only |
| `safety/teal` | deep teal | Protected Help and verified safety states |

Large lime backgrounds are prohibited. Lime is an interaction signal, not a section colour.

### 3.2 Typography

- heavy condensed or extended grotesk for dominant display words;
- expressive italic serif for the contrasting phrase inside display headlines;
- modern extended sans for navigation, body, evidence and controls;
- desktop display scale approximately 112–176px depending on composition;
- section statements approximately 56–88px;
- body copy generally 16–20px and limited to one or two short sentences.

The mixed display treatment must express one semantic thought; it must not become decorative word swapping.

### 3.3 Geometry and depth

- 1440px desktop canvas with a 1280px working width;
- full-viewport chapters of approximately 760–920px;
- 20–28px radius on editorial image and product cards;
- pill actions and compact utility controls;
- no glass, glow or generic gradient decoration;
- minimal shadows; hierarchy comes from scale, crop, contrast and spatial overlap;
- partially visible neighbouring cards may signal horizontal exploration without hiding required information.

## 4. Home architecture

The production Home SHALL use the following Tilt-derived sequence:

1. **Cinematic Hero** — persistent navigation, oversized mixed-type promise, four editorial image crops, one `Start the 10-Step Program` action and one short supporting line.
2. **Product Theatre** — a horizontal stage centred on Mission 01, with adjacent previews of the Personal Control Dashboard and 10-Step path.
3. **Statement Break** — `Is gambling becoming harder to control?` with three short static signs and no input, score or diagnosis.
4. **Human Chapter: Recognise** — full-bleed photo, one short method explanation and no new destination.
5. **Human Chapter: Build** — full-bleed photo, one short explanation of pause rules and limits.
6. **Human Chapter: Apply** — full-bleed positive photo, one short explanation of using and reviewing the plan.
7. **Tools Working for You** — three object-led cards for Trigger Map, Pause Rule and Review Routine.
8. **Evidence and Momentum** — source evidence, clinical limitation, programme facts and only approved testimonial evidence. Before an evidence pack exists, use factual programme metrics and neutral illustrative plan-use examples rather than fabricated reviews.
9. **Final Program Action** — repeat `Start the 10-Step Program`, followed directly by the shared Footer whose `CONTROL & SUPPORT` block links to the protected Help route.
10. **Footer** — commercial discovery, product, methodology, legal, privacy, Help and affiliate disclosure.

## 5. Copy and action contract

- The Home has one conversion destination: `Start the 10-Step Program`.
- The action may be repeated in the persistent header, Hero and final action block.
- Lower chapters use no new primary destination.
- Each full-screen chapter has one headline and at most one short supporting sentence.
- The self-recognition signs remain static and non-diagnostic.
- The exact limitation remains visible: `SevenBet draws on methods studied in gambling-harm treatment and behaviour-change research. The complete programme has not yet been clinically evaluated.`
- No claim may guarantee control, recovery, reduced gambling or clinical improvement.

## 6. Imagery contract

- Hero media shows adult agency, presence and everyday life rather than sadness, shame or a staged crisis.
- Photography must be original, generated or properly licensed and must not reuse Tilt assets.
- Crops should feel editorial and close, with people occupying the frame confidently.
- Demographic selection must follow applicable market evidence and inclusive art direction; race must not be inferred as a behavioural targeting signal.
- Positive imagery may illustrate programme use but must not imply a participant result without the required evidence pack.
- Product and object renders must communicate real SevenBet functions rather than generic decoration.

## 7. Component and motion contract

Required reusable components:

- persistent public navigation;
- acid primary button and dark ghost action;
- mixed-display heading composition;
- editorial media tile;
- product-theatre card and carousel controls;
- static recognition sign;
- full-bleed human chapter;
- object-led tool card;
- evidence/metric card;
- protected Help access in the Public Header and Footer, with a prominent panel reserved for approved urgent/protected contexts;
- dark legal footer.

Motion should provide section transitions, card focus, subtle image crop movement, progress and saved-state confirmation. Reduced-motion equivalents are required. Motion must not create gambling-like anticipation or rewards.

## 8. Figma delivery order

1. preserve earlier Human Guidance work as a superseded audit artefact until the Tilt-locked replacement is accepted;
2. create a separate Tilt-Locked foundation proposal;
3. build Core components before the Home;
4. build SevenBet-specific product, media, chapter and evidence components;
5. assemble and validate the continuous desktop Home;
6. validate the Home against the Tilt reference screenshots for composition, scale, crop, rhythm and copy density;
7. create the mobile interpretation;
8. move accepted artefacts to Ready for Dev and deliberately archive superseded work.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

- the result is recognisably derived from Tilt's composition mechanics without copying Tilt's brand assets;
- the Hero is a cinematic editorial composition, not a split-layout landing template;
- display typography, photography and negative space create the primary visual impact;
- the Program is visible as an active product inside a high-impact product theatre;
- the page contains materially less explanatory copy than RFC-006 explorations;
- lime is limited to interactive emphasis and never becomes a large aggressive surface;
- self-recognition, evidence limitations and protected Help access remain clear;
- commercial discovery stays accessible in header and footer without entering the harm-recognition narrative;
- desktop and mobile preserve the same narrative order;
- no Tilt logo, copy, photo, testimonial or proprietary product render is reused.

## 10. Consequences

This direction raises media, typography, motion and frontend implementation requirements. It also provides the emotional impact and consumer confidence missing from Human Guidance. SevenBet will need a stronger licensed or generated media library, custom display typography, real product renders and deliberate scroll choreography. The higher craft cost is accepted because the site must persuade operators and users that SevenBet is a serious consumer product before commercial partnerships begin.

## 12. Article-detail system amendment — 2026-08-04

SevenBet SHALL replace the current card-stacked Learning Center article presentation with a reusable Tilt-Locked editorial article template. The template preserves the detected backend content model — category, title, summary, reading time, author, editor, update date, takeaways, sections, examples, FAQ, related articles and next reading — while presenting it as continuous long-form reading rather than a sequence of generic cards.

The reference lock for article detail pages is the human-scale editorial rhythm observed on Tilt's About page: one dominant thesis, rare but meaningful human media, numbered narrative chapters, strong typographic pauses and short proof blocks. Medium contributes reading-width discipline only. Health-information patterns contribute visible author/editor/update metadata, evidence sources and a protected support route. These secondary references must not dilute the active Tilt-Locked colour, typography, geometry or component roles.

The article template has two content modes:

1. **Educational-commercial** — neutral articles about bonus terms, casino evaluation, licensing, payments and comparison methodology may show contextual internal links to Casinos, Bonuses, Best Offers or reviews after the educational answer. Links must be relevant, disclosed and useful without an affiliate relationship.
2. **Protected-control** — articles about stopping gambling, loss of control, self-exclusion, gambling-related harm or urgent support must not contain casino, bonus, offer or affiliate actions in the body, related-reading rail or final action. These articles may route only to the 10-Step Program, neutral educational resources and the protected Help experience.

Commercial links are therefore conditional rather than universal. Vulnerability, self-check, Program, Help, pause or control-plan data must not select, rank or personalise commercial article links.

The v1 protected-control article detail SHALL contain:

- persistent public header, with commercial discovery remaining globally available but visually subordinate to reading;
- article hero with category, plain-language title, one-sentence summary, reading time, reviewed date and editorial ownership;
- one original or properly licensed human image illustrating agency and everyday life rather than distress or a promised outcome;
- a short direct answer and three numbered action chapters;
- a practical first-plan block that turns reading into an explicit next action;
- visible evidence and source links, including applicable NHS, NICE and Gambling Commission material;
- related educational reading, FAQ and one Program action;
- the existing public Footer and disclosures, including the `CONTROL & SUPPORT` protected-Help link.

Medical, therapeutic or guaranteed-control claims are prohibited. Educational copy must distinguish SevenBet's editorial synthesis from clinical evaluation, keep source dates visible and undergo content and compliance review before production publication.

## 13. Public Help access hierarchy amendment — 2026-08-05

The approved public-screen composition SHALL keep protected Help continuously available without placing a separate high-emphasis `Need support now?` panel after the final Programme or commercial action. On standard public acquisition, commercial-discovery and educational screens, the large standalone Protected Help panel is removed from the page sequence. This amendment overrides the standalone-panel requirement in sections 4, 7, 9 and 12 where those sections conflict with this access hierarchy.

Protected Help access remains mandatory through both parts of the approved Public Shell:

- the persistent Public Header keeps its `Help` route;
- the Public Footer `CONTROL & SUPPORT` block keeps the neutral support explanation and adds an explicit `Open Help` link to `/responsible-gambling`.

The footer link is a compact tertiary utility action, not a second primary CTA. The final `Start the 10-Step Program` or `Start Mission 01` action remains the dominant end-of-content action. `safety/teal` remains reserved for the compact support surface and verified safety states; it must not become a large public-page attention surface.

This amendment changes public composition only. It does not change the protected route, Help eligibility, the prohibition on casino, bonus or affiliate calls to action inside protected Help, or the separation of Program, pause, Help and vulnerability data from commercial targeting and personalisation. A prominent standalone safety treatment remains permissible only in an approved urgent-support or protected-Help context whose content contract requires it; it is not the default ending for public screens.
